# Weapon Cleaner

The Weapon Cleaner repairs one item that DayZ considers compatible with a Weapon Cleaning Kit.

## Capacity and compatibility

Use exactly one of these locations:

- one rifle attachment;
- one pistol attachment; or
- one compatible cargo item, such as a magazine.

An item that is not configured for vanilla weapon-kit repair will not work, even if it looks like a weapon part.

## Requirements

- Deployed Weapon Cleaner
- Cleaning Solvent with at least 20 quantity
- Cleaning Patches with at least 20 quantity
- One non-ruined compatible item

## Operation

1. Open the Weapon Cleaner.
2. Attach Cleaning Solvent and Cleaning Patches.
3. Add one compatible item.
4. Start the 26-second cleaning action.

A completed cycle consumes 20 solvent and 20 patch quantity and sets the item to `WeaponCleanerRepairedHealth`. `EnableWeaponCleanerSound` controls only the operating sound.

## Key classnames

```text
rag_baseitems_weapon_cleaner_kit
rag_baseitems_weapon_solvent
rag_baseitems_cleaning_patches
```
