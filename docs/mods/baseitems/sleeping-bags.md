# Sleeping Bags

RaG Baseitems includes seven deployable and packable sleeping-bag designs. In the current source they are placeable objects with a pack action; they do **not** add a custom sleep, spawn, or respawn system.

## Use

1. Place the packed sleeping bag.
2. Use **Pack tent** on the deployed bag to turn it back into its matching packed item.

Item properties transfer when it is packed.

## Packed classnames

The source intentionally uses the spelling `ckeckered` in these classnames. Do not correct it in economy or trader files.

```text
rag_baseitems_sleepingbag_packed_ckeckered_blue
rag_baseitems_sleepingbag_packed_ckeckered_brown
rag_baseitems_sleepingbag_packed_ckeckered_green
rag_baseitems_sleepingbag_packed_ckeckered_grey
```
