# Clothing try-on and weapon builder

Both previews use offers available at the current trader and in the selected currency. Prices and stock are checked again when you buy.

## Clothing try-on

Select a buyable clothing listing and choose **Try On**. The preview starts with your visible equipment. Choose a category or slot, then add clothing from the trader. Headgear and helmets can appear in separate categories even though choosing one replaces the other on the model.

Use the outfit list to see each selected garment and its price. Remove a choice to show the equipment you already wear in that slot. **Buy** submits the selected pieces in one checkout. Purchased items go through normal delivery, so equip them from inventory after buying.

## Weapon builder

Select a buyable weapon and choose **Build Weapon**. The preview starts with the weapon and its included attachments. Add compatible parts offered at the trader, such as magazines, optics, lights, muzzle devices, or other parts. Conflicting parts replace one another in the preview.

The price list shows the base weapon and each selected paid attachment. Included parts are already in the weapon price. You can add up to 12 paid parts. Check the preview, stats, and total, then buy the assembled weapon in one checkout. Assembly must succeed for delivery to complete. Modded weapon behavior can differ from the preview.

Read [purchase contents](ammo-liquids-and-purchase-contents.md) to check whether a magazine includes rounds or an accessory includes a battery.
