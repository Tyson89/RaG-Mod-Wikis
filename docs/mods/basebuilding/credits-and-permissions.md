## Monetization and repacking

This mod may not be repacked, reuploaded, or monetized directly. It may be used on a Bohemia Interactive-approved monetized server as long as the mod itself is not part of the monetization.

Repacking permission is not granted.

See [Bohemia Interactive's monetization rules](https://www.bohemia.net/monetization) for the platform requirements.
